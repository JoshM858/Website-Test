How it works

Three steps. No downloads to chase, no accounts to manage.

01

Get an invite

Your tutor sends your cohort a short invite code at the start of semester.

02

Join your room

Enter the code once. You land straight in your cohort's shared space — no one else can see it.

03

Study together

Build the question bank, run a round before class, or just drop into a session the night before.

Access

Recall isn't public — and that's the point.

Every room is tied to a real cohort. No open sign-up, no strangers, no noise. If your course is running it, your tutor already has your code.
