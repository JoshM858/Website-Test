Cookie Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This site does not use cookies. Not essential ones, not analytics, not advertising — none. That's why there's no cookie banner here: putting one up would imply a choice you don't actually need to make.

What are cookies, briefly

Cookies are small pieces of data a website can ask your browser to store, typically used to remember who you are between visits, track behaviour for analytics or advertising, or keep you logged in. This site does none of that, so it sets none.

What this site actually does

This is a static page. When you visit it:

  • No cookies are set, first-party or third-party
  • No browser local storage or session storage is used
  • No analytics or advertising scripts run (no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar)
  • Fonts are loaded from this site's own server, not a third-party font service, so no separate request (and no cookie) is sent to a company like Google as a side effect of rendering text

The only external requests this page makes are to load three open-source JavaScript libraries (GSAP and Lenis for animation, D3 for the interactive topic map) from a public code CDN. These are plain script files — loading them doesn't set a cookie or transmit anything about you. The home page's background video is served from this site itself.

If that ever changes

If a future version of this site adds analytics or any other cookie-setting functionality, a proper consent banner — asking before anything is set, not just notifying after the fact — would be added at that point, along with an update to this page and the Privacy Policy.
